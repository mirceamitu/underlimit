Auto measures each page and sends ink-on-paper down the black & white path, photographs down the grey or colour path.

Kept as-is

Page count, page order and page size come out exactly as they went in, so stamps and signatures stay where they were placed. Nothing is added to the file: no metadata, no text layer.

How it gets there
Read

Every page, measured for real

Each page is rendered here and encoded every way it could be sent, so the size you see is the size you download.

Sort

Text pages away from photos

A scan of typed paper only needs black and white. A photograph needs grey or colour. Treating them alike wastes the budget.

Trim

Down to the limit, then stop

Uneven lighting is flattened, then the page is reduced to ink and paper and packed with Group 4, the compression fax machines use for exactly this.

Check

Nothing silently dropped

If the limit can't be met above your resolution floor, it says so and shows the closest fit instead of quietly wrecking the document.
